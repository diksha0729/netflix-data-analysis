# Netflix Data Exploration & Visualization

## 📌 Business Problem
Netflix has over 8,000 titles. The objective of this project is to analyze the content library to determine:
1. What type of content Netflix should produce.
2. How content distribution varies across different countries.

## 📊 Key Insights
- **Type Distribution:** ~70% of titles are Movies (6,131) and ~30% are TV Shows (2,676).
- **TV Show Trends:** 67% of TV shows have only 1 season, but TV show additions have grown steadily since 2018.
- **Geographic Production:** The US leads total content (~42%), followed by India (mostly Movies) and South Korea/Japan (mostly TV Shows).
- **Duration:** The median movie duration is 98 minutes, while kids' movies (TV-Y, TV-Y7) are significantly shorter.

## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries Used
- **Python 3.12**
- **Pandas & NumPy** (Data Cleaning & Un-nesting)
- **Seaborn & Matplotlib** (Data Visualization)
